Iya Bolaji

means "Bolaji's mother" in Yoruba.

Food like your mum made it

That is the whole idea behind the kitchen: food that tastes like someone's mum cooked it for you, served hot, with enough pepper to make you sit up.

On 3rd Avenue Road in Ipaja the pots go from jollof and ofada to amala with ewedu and gbegiri, efo riro and egusi, and peppered turkey, bokoto and asun.

Eat with us, take it home, or have it delivered. Either way, belle go full.
